About

threat level: human is an independent project publishing free, plain-language guides to the human side of cybersecurity: how people get exploited through social engineering, phishing, and related attacks, and the specific red flags that give each one away. Calm and practical, no hype.

The field guides are free to read online, download, print, and share. No signup, no tracking, no ads.
